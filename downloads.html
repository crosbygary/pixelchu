READY IN A FEW TAPS

From download
to play.

  1. Save a cartridge to Files.

    Choose your console above. If you downloaded the ZIP, tap it in Files to extract it first.

  2. Open Pixelchu → My games.

    Tap Import a game, then select the cartridge file. Pixelchu copies it into your local library.

  3. Move. Tap. Try again.

    Test the D-pad, A, and Start. The same demos are also included in the app under Try a little homebrew.
