Privacy Policy

Updated 1 November 2026

Pixelchu is designed for offline play. This policy explains the information handled by the iPhone app, this website, and support email.

Who to contact

Pixelchu is maintained by its independent developer. For privacy questions or requests, contact cr0sby_gar@icloud.com.

Your games stay in your app

Imported ROMs, library information, battery saves, save states, favorites, and settings are stored locally in Pixelchu's app container. Pixelchu does not upload these files or operate a cloud-sync service. The app uses Apple's Files picker to read only files you select; those files may come from iCloud Drive or another file provider you use.

Your device or computer backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. File providers and backup services handle their own copies under their own policies.

No tracking or advertising SDKs

The current app has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking identifiers, or developer-operated telemetry endpoint. Pixelchu does not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. It does not ask for access to your contacts, location, microphone, or photos.

Apple may separately process App Store, device, or diagnostic information according to your settings and Apple's privacy policy. This is separate from Pixelchu's own data handling.

This website and external links

This static website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, local storage, or embedded third-party fonts. Its appearance follows your device's light or dark setting.

The site is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Hosting requests, including requests for downloadable ROMs, are processed by GitHub under its privacy statement. Opening an external reference from the app or website also sends a request to that website; its own privacy policy applies.

When you email us

If you contact cr0sby_gar@icloud.com, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, troubleshoot, and handle your request. Email is processed by our email provider, Apple iCloud Mail, and yours. Please do not send ROMs, passwords, payment information, or other unnecessary sensitive data.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the issue and maintain necessary support or legal records. You can request deletion by email; information that must be retained by law may be kept for that purpose.

Your choices and requests

You can remove a game and its associated saves from My games. Deleting Pixelchu from your iPhone removes its local app container; offloading the app may retain its data. Manage device backups and original ROM copies separately in the relevant Apple or file-provider settings.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the handling of personal information and to complain to your local privacy authority. Contact the email above for information we hold in support correspondence. We cannot retrieve your local game library from our systems because it is not sent to us.

Children and changes

Pixelchu does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. If a child has sent personal information by email, a parent or guardian can contact us to request deletion.

If our data practices change, we will update this policy and its revision date, and provide any additional notice or consent required for that change.
