Before you write

How do I import a game?

Put a lawfully obtained, uncompressed ROM in Files. In My games, tap Import a game and select it. Accepted extensions: .gb, .gbc, .gba, .nes, .sfc and .smc. Extract ZIP files first.

Where can I get a test ROM?

Our Pocket Meadow downloads are original, MIT-licensed movement tests. They are also included in the app.

Where are my saves?

Pixelchu stores saves in its private app container. It does not provide cloud sync. Deleting a game removes its local saves, and deleting the app removes its local data. Your device backups are managed separately by Apple.
